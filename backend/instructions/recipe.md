1. Call read_results to see how the current version is doing: its development mistakes, its weakest notices and our reviewers' corrections since the last round.
2. Look at the model and reasoning effort of the current version and of the version each earlier round tried. If one of the models allowed, at one of its reasoning efforts, hasn't been used by any of them, try it this round, starting with a higher reasoning effort on the current model. Keep the instructions and the parameters as they are, so the round shows what the model and reasoning effort alone change, cite the cases whose mistakes it should mend, and go to step 4.
3. Otherwise, weigh the reviewers' corrections beside the development mistakes and find what most of them share. Make the narrowest general change that would mend it, keeping the same model and reasoning effort: a rule in the instructions, or a parameter within its bounds when the false exceptions are rounding differences of a cent or two.
4. Call propose_change with the new configuration, its reason and the ids of the cases behind it.
5. Call run_evaluation to test the new version on both sets.
6. Improve this recipe, so that the next round makes a better change. Look at what happened in this round and in earlier rounds, then call propose_recipe with the new recipe. Keep this as the last step of the new recipe.
